# 05. Bidding Instructions and Contract Terms
**Context:** This document outlines the administrative rules for bidding on this project and the legal stipulations regarding invoicing, force majeure, and penalties.

## 1. Tender Submission Rules
*   **Project Title:** Partnership for Energy Efficiency in Buildings (PEEB Cool)
*   **Tender Reference:** 7000004975 / Design, Development and Operationalization of an Interactive Energy Balance Dashboard
*   **Submission Deadline:** 05th May 2026.
*   **Submission Method:** A strict Two-Email Procedure to `LK_Services_Quotation@giz.de`:
    *   **Email 1 (Technical Proposal):** Must contain the Methodological Concept, Company Profile, and CVs. *NO pricing information allowed.*
    *   **Email 2 (Financial Proposal):** Price Quotation in LKR (exclusive of VAT), signed, with bank details.
*   **Validity:** Bids must remain valid for 60 days after submission.
*   **Questions:** Queries must be sent via email between 07.04.2026 and 30.04.2026.

## 2. Financial Terms and Remuneration
*   **Fixed Pricing:** The agreed contract price is a maximum amount. Exceeding costs will not be reimbursed.
*   **Invoicing:** Fees are calculated based on verifiable "expert-days." Subcontracts and travel expenses (if approved) require original receipts and documentation.
*   **Final Payment:** The final invoice must be submitted within 6 weeks of completing the assignment. Payment occurs within 30 days of receiving the final invoice and formal acceptance of the system.
*   **Security Deposit:** For work contracts, a 10% retention fee from invoices may apply until final system acceptance is achieved.

## 3. Delays and Penalties
*   **Contractual Penalties:** Failure to meet agreed delivery deadlines will result in a penalty of 0.5% of the total remuneration per week of delay. The maximum penalty is capped at 8% of the total contract value.
*   **Breach of Integrity:** Bribes or severe conflicts of interest incur a separate penalty reaching up to EUR 50,000, or matching the bribe amount if higher, alongside immediate termination.

## 4. Force Majeure and Interruption
*   **Force Majeure:** Includes natural disasters, war, pandemics, etc. Both parties may suspend obligations. If the delay exceeds 3 months, either party may terminate the contract.
*   **Client Suspension:** GIZ reserves the right to halt work for political or strategic reasons. Contractors will be reimbursed for completed work and reasonable incurred expenses up to that date, but cannot claim full unearned contract values.
