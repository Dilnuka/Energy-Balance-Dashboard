# 04. Team Structure and Evaluation Grid
**Context:** This document details the personnel required to build the system and how the bidding proposals are technically evaluated.

## 1. Required Team Roles & Qualifications
The implementation team must consist of 5 core experts. Fluency in English is mandatory for all, with Sinhala/Tamil fluency considered an advantage.

### Expert 1: Business Analyst / Requirements Analyst
*   **Minimum Experience:** 6 years.
*   **Responsibilities:** Translating sectoral energy data requirements into functional/technical specifications and assessing business intelligence needs.

### Expert 2: System Architect / Lead Software Developer
*   **Minimum Experience:** 6 years.
*   **Responsibilities:** High-level system architecture, database-driven application design, and leading backend/frontend development.

### Expert 3: Software Developer (Backend/Frontend/Data)
*   **Minimum Experience:** 4 years.
*   **Responsibilities:** Developing the data processing modules, computational engine, APIs, interactive dashboards, and RBAC implementation.

### Expert 4: UI/UX Designer & User Guidance Specialist
*   **Minimum Experience:** 3 years.
*   **Responsibilities:** Designing accessible wireframes, responsive dashboards, data visualizations, and crafting user manuals.

### Expert 5: QA/QC, Data Validation & Training Specialist
*   **Minimum Experience:** 5 years.
*   **Responsibilities:** Conducting security, functional, and user acceptance testing. Ensuring data auditability and conducting SLSEA staff training.

---

## 2. Technical Evaluation Grid (Weighting Matrix)
Proposals are assessed using a 70% Technical / 30% Financial split. The 70% Technical portion is scored out of a maximum of 1000 points, broken down as follows:

### Part A: Qualifications of Proposed Staff (45% of Technical Score)
*   **Expert 1:** 12% (Education: 3%, Professional Exp: 5%, Regional Exp: 3%, Language: 1%)
*   **Expert 2:** 12% (Education: 3%, Professional Exp: 5%, Regional Exp: 3%, Language: 1%)
*   **Expert 3:** 10% (Education: 3%, Professional Exp: 5%, Regional Exp: 1%, Language: 1%)
*   **Expert 4:** 5% (Education: 1%, Professional Exp: 2%, Regional Exp: 1%, Language: 1%)
*   **Expert 5:** 6% (Education: 2%, Professional Exp: 2%, Regional Exp: 1%, Language: 1%)

### Part B: Proposed Methodology & Architecture (55% of Technical Score)
*   **Design Brief & Architecture (10%):** Appropriateness of the proposed software architecture and response to requirements.
*   **Timeline & Approach (10%):** Realism and depth of the development methodology and milestone planning.
*   **Technologies & Platforms (10%):** Suitability of the proposed databases, UI/UX tools, and frameworks.
*   **QA & Security (10%):** Robustness of user acceptance testing, backups, and security protocols.
*   **Previous Experience (15%):** Track record in developing similar enterprise, data-heavy systems for public sector clients.
